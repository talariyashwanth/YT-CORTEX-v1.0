# University Regulations

## Attendance Policy

Students are required to maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each course to be eligible for end-semester examinations.

Students who fall below 75% attendance may be denied examination eligibility unless granted a formal exemption by the academic committee.

## Scholarship Guidelines

Scholarship applications must be submitted by March 21 each academic year. Required documents include academic transcripts, income certificate, and a completed application form.

Merit scholarships require a minimum CGPA of 8.0. Need-based scholarships are evaluated separately based on family income documentation.

## Examination Eligibility

To sit for end-semester examinations, students must:
1. Maintain minimum 75% attendance
2. Complete all mandatory assignments
3. Have no outstanding disciplinary actions
4. Pay all applicable fees before the examination deadline

Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation through the academic office.

## Academic Integrity

Plagiarism, cheating, and unauthorized collaboration are prohibited. Violations may result in course failure and disciplinary action.

All submitted work must be the student's own unless group work is explicitly permitted.
